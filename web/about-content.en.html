Game guide

HALLS is a first-person Backrooms maze game that runs in your terminal. Explore randomized rooms, avoid roaming entities, and find the exit.

How you reach the exit is up to you.

All footage below shows demonstration scenes.

Exploration

Yellow wallpaper, carpet, fluorescent lights, pillars, and interconnected passages form the maze. The layout and your starting position change with each new game.

Walking gives you time to look around; running moves you faster and makes louder footsteps. Ambient hum, footsteps, and pursuit sounds change with the action.

Coins

Coins can be found in the maze. Collect them to increase your score.

Flashlight and battery

Press F to light up dark areas. The bottom-right corner shows whether the flashlight is on and how much charge remains.

The battery drains while the light is on and recharges automatically after switching it off. An empty battery turns the light off.

Entities

Black, wire-like entities with elongated bodies and limbs roam through the rooms and passages. They investigate footsteps and pursue players they notice.

An enraged entity visibly trembles and is surrounded by black particles.

Damage and death

Entities attack at close range. A hit knocks you back and shakes your view. When your health runs out, your character falls and you can start a new run.

The exit

Green flooring and green particles mark the exit. Step into it to complete your run.

Results and replays

After reaching the exit, enter a name and press Enter to save your proof in the game folder.

Submit the .hproof file here. Approved public runs can enter the leaderboard, and other players can watch your replay.

Settings

The menu lets you adjust the rendering multiplier. Higher values improve image detail and require more processing power. Volume can be adjusted during play.
